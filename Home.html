How it breaks down
Supports + - * / ^, parentheses, and sin cos tan ln sqrt exp, plus implicit multiplication like 2x or 3sin(x). Derivatives cover the sum, product, quotient, power, and chain rules. Integrals cover sums, constant multiples, the power rule, and substitution for linear insides, like sin(2x) or (2x+1)^4. Products and quotients of two functions, and non-linear substitutions, aren't attempted — Tangent will say so rather than guess.
